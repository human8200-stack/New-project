How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @maison_edel_ on Instagram saying "Please delete my data."
  2. We will delete any comment, message, or reply draft we hold about you (including drafts in our private Notion workspace) within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What you can do yourself

  • Delete your comments on @maison_edel_ posts directly in Instagram.
  • Delete or unsend your direct messages in Instagram.
  • If you ever connected an app named "Maison Edel Studio" to your own account, remove it in Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Maison Edel Studio → Remove.

Maison Edel Studio does not keep its own database of your data. Comments and messages remain on Instagram and are governed by Instagram's own data policy.
