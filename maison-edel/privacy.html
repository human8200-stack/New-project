Who we are

Maison Edel is a cooking and lifestyle creator brand that publishes on Instagram as @maison_edel_. The app "Maison Edel Studio" is a private tool used only by Maison Edel to manage its own Instagram account. It is not offered to other businesses or users.

What information we access

Through the Instagram API, the app accesses only information related to the @maison_edel_ account:

  • Comments left on @maison_edel_ posts, including the commenter's Instagram username and the comment text and time
  • Direct messages sent to @maison_edel_, including the sender's Instagram username, Instagram-scoped ID, and message text and time
  • Basic profile information and media of the @maison_edel_ account itself

We do not collect passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, locations, or payment information.

How we use it

  • To read and reply to comments and direct messages sent to @maison_edel_
  • To publish and schedule Maison Edel's own posts

Reply drafts may be prepared with the help of an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic). Every reply is reviewed and approved by a person at Maison Edel before it is sent. Drafts awaiting approval are kept in a private Notion workspace that only Maison Edel can access.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not use it for advertising or share it with third parties, except the service providers listed above (Meta/Instagram, Anthropic, Notion) solely to operate the features described here, or where required by law.

Retention

The app does not keep its own database of your comments or messages; they remain on Instagram. Reply drafts in Notion are deleted within 30 days after the reply is sent or discarded.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete your own comments and messages on Instagram at any time. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you, see Data deletion instructions.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Send a direct message to @maison_edel_ on Instagram.
